# Code of conduct

We aim to make participation respectful, constructive, and welcoming. Be considerate of differing backgrounds and experience; critique ideas and code, not people; and accept good-faith corrections.

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, doxxing, and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors. For private concerns, contact repository maintainers through the private channel documented in the repository hosting service; do not post security or personal-data reports publicly.
